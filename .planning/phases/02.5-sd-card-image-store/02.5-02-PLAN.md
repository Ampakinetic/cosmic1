<objective>
Crash-resume slice: undelivered images survive reboot and power loss. A boot-time rescan of the card rebuilds a small RAM index of undelivered files, re-announces them through the EXISTING push/manifest/window machinery, and persists delivery state so delivered images are never re-announced (ROADMAP STORE-02; success criterion 3: "Reboot mid-transfer resumes: boot rescan re-announces the undelivered file and the base completes it — no re-capture needed").

Purpose: the volatile queue lost every queued buffer at reboot (G-01-6 class); the file-backed archive turns reboot from data-loss into resume.

Output: bootRescan() + delivery bookkeeping in the balloon SD module, resume admission in ImageTxManager, setup wiring. Wire unchanged; builds green.
</objective>
