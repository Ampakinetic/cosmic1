Close the balloon command-path findings routed for this round: CR-04 (the blanket camera-ready gate in CommandHandler::executeCommand refuses every command during camera-down windows, blocking image retrieval and status exactly when they matter) and the carried WR-03 (a manual capture never advances the auto-capture interval baseline, so an interval capture can fire moments after a manual one).

Purpose: re-verification #7 confirmed CR-04 at source — executeCommand (src/command_handler.cpp:139-145) rejects EVERY command with NACK_BUSY when camera->isReady() is false, and isReady() is just 'initialized' (camera_manager.h:134). On low battery, main_balloon.cpp:864 calls enableCamera(false) -> end() -> initialized=false while ImageTx().process() keeps running with buffers and radio fully operational — from that moment every IMAGE_WINDOW_REQUEST is refused (announced fulls unretrievable until power loss or TTL destroys them; the final images an operator needs most), and GET_STATUS is refused while the balloon still beacons. The refusal reason (camera) is unrelated to the resource those commands need. WR-03 is the review's carried warning: manual captures bypass the AutoCapture baseline, so the interval timer can fire a second capture seconds after a manual trigger — the verifier named the fix ("markCaptureBaseline() after successful manual capture") and routed it into this round.
