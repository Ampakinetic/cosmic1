## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-08 | Tampering | handleAutoCaptureEnable interval | medium | mitigate | Interval re-validated in AutoCapture::enable (1000..3600000 ms) even if handler validation is bypassed — the timer cannot be driven into a radio-saturating loop below 1 Hz |
| T-01-09 | DoS | AutoCapture::process + camera->captureImage | medium | mitigate | Captures are spaced at minimum 1000 ms by the validated interval; a failed capture does not reset the baseline early (baseline updates before the attempt), preventing a tight failure loop |
| T-01-10 | Repudiation | image ID sequence | low | accept | Single uint16 counter shared by manual and automatic captures, wraps at 65535; Phase 2 image sequencing owns durable IDs |
</threat_model>
